1. **Initial Consultation**: We'll discuss your challenges, goals, and current setup
2. **Project Proposal**: A detailed plan with timeline, deliverables, and investment
3. **Collaborative Development**: Regular check-ins and feedback throughout the project

- **Process Optimization**: Workflow mapping and automation implementation
- **Custom Software**: Web applications and data processing systems
- **Data Infrastructure**: Database design, cloud migration, and system integration
- **Analytics & Reporting**: Automated reporting and business intelligence solutions
