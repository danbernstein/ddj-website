case_studies:
  - title: "Legal Services Intake Pathway Redesign"
    challenge: "A multi-office legal aid organization with 120+ staff had fragmented intake processes across three channels (online, phone, clinic). Managing attorneys struggled with inconsistent case acceptance data, unclear transfer workflows, and high follow-up burden. Staff couldn't identify whether intake gaps or capacity constraints were driving rejection patterns."
    solution:
      - "Conducted 30+ structured interviews with intake specialists, attorneys, and supervisors across multiple offices"
      - "Mapped current intake workflows from first contact through case acceptance, including transfer and escalation points"
      - "Analyzed LegalServer data and call system metrics to identify bottlenecks and decision-point delays"
      - "Assessed alignment between case acceptance guidelines, funding requirements, and actual caseload distribution"
      - "Documented pain points and developed recommendations for triage refinement and staffing alignment"
    results:
      - text: "Clarified intake decision criteria that reduced downstream follow-up work"
      - text: "Identified 5 high-impact workflow improvements with measurable impact on staff time"
      - text: "Provided implementation roadmap with sequencing and dependencies"
      - text: "Built staff confidence in process changes through collaborative design"
    technologies: "LegalServer analysis, Vonage call data, structured interviews, workflow mapping diagrams, RACI matrices"

  - title: "Homeless Services Data Ecosystem Assessment"
    challenge: "A state housing agency managing a system used by 24+ service providers was struggling with disconnected workflows. Outreach workers used paper forms and Smartsheet duplicatively. Case managers lacked visibility into client caseloads. Intake and exit processes had gaps. Stakeholders disagreed on whether the core system needed features, replacement, or integration with other tools."
    solution:
      - "Conducted 28 interviews with 46 stakeholders across providers to understand user needs by role and geography"
      - "Developed personas for outreach workers, case managers, and coordinators to understand actual workflows"
      - "Mapped client journey from outreach through housing placement and exit follow-up"
      - "Categorized 200+ feedback points into themes: data entry, user experience, reporting, system reliability, data sharing"
      - "Benchmarked against 7 other state/CoC models (Nevada, Allegheny County, Boston, Austin) to understand platform options and cost"
    results:
      - text: "Synthesized provider needs into clear decision framework for technology strategy"
      - text: "Identified that issues were primarily UX and integration, not core system replacement"
      - text: "Provided comparative analysis of platform options with implementation cost and staffing implications"
      - text: "Equipped agency leadership to align stakeholders on next steps"
    technologies: "Interview analysis, persona development, process flow mapping, competitive benchmarking, heat maps of user pain points"

  - title: "Case Management Workflow Standardization"
    challenge: "A legal services organization had grown to multiple offices with inconsistent practices around case assignment, document management, and handoffs between intake and case handling. Each office maintained separate SharePoint repositories. Staff couldn't easily locate templates or prior examples. New attorneys struggled to onboard. Reporting couldn't distinguish between operational inconsistencies and legitimate regional differences."
    solution:
      - "Mapped case management workflows from assignment through closure across three offices"
      - "Documented where processes diverged and why (legitimate regional needs vs. silos)"
      - "Assessed template library usage and identified gaps in high-volume case types"
      - "Designed governance structure for document management with clear ownership and update responsibilities"
      - "Developed standardized naming conventions and folder structures for SharePoint"
    results:
      - text: "Reduced onboarding time for new staff by consolidating scattered procedures"
      - text: "Enabled better internal referrals by clarifying which offices had subject-matter specialists"
      - text: "Provided template governance process to prevent document drift"
      - text: "Improved data consistency for reporting and funder compliance"
    technologies: "SharePoint audits, workflow documentation, governance frameworks, process improvement checklists"
