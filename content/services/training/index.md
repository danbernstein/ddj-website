  - title: "SharePoint Organization & Document Management Training"
    challenge: "A legal services organization had implemented SharePoint but staff were confused about how to use it effectively. Multiple versions of the same template existed across different staff members' folders. New attorneys couldn't find relevant prior work. Each office organized their SharePoint differently, making cross-office collaboration difficult. Staff spent time searching for documents or recreating work that already existed."
    solution:
      - "Audited current SharePoint usage to understand how staff actually organized and found documents"
      - "Documented naming conventions, folder structures, and policies that would make documents discoverable"
      - "Developed guidelines distinguishing between personal storage (individual OneDrive) and shared storage (team SharePoint)"
      - "Conducted training sessions with legal and office staff to introduce new standards"
   # results:
   #   - text: "Standardized SharePoint structure across offices, reducing confusion"
   #   - text: "Staff spent less time searching for documents through clearer organization"
   #   - text: "Reduced duplicate files and versions through shared naming conventions"

    technologies: "SharePoint, training workshops"
